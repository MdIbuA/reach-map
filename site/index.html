      The catch

      • The colours are an estimate. Each hexagon uses straight-line distance × 1.35 for street detours, at 3.5 km/h. Both numbers are the medians of 35 real walks from Amazon Location Service, so the estimate matches what you get when you click. The click is the ground truth.
      • Map data has gaps. Overture Places misses some government clinics and many small shops, and "hospital" includes private nursing homes. Places with confidence below 0.5 or marked closed are dropped.
      • Transit means stations, not every MTC bus stop. Roadside stops aren't in the data.
      • Reach isn't access. A clinic 5 minutes away might be full, expensive or closed at night.

      Data · Overture Maps places (2026-10-21.0) and Meta & CIESIN HRSL population, both read from S3 via the Registry of Open Data on AWS.
