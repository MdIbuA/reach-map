      The catch

      • The colours are an estimate. Each hexagon uses straight-line distance × 1.35 for street detours, at 3.5 km/h. Both numbers are the medians of 35 real walks from Amazon Location Service, so the estimate matches the walks you get when you click. Clicking replaces the guess with the real walking area.
      • Map data has gaps. Overture Places comes from open and business listings. It misses some government clinics and many small shops, and "hospital" includes private nursing homes. I kept places with confidence ≥ 0.5 and dropped ones marked closed.
      • Transit means stations, not every MTC bus stop. Roadside bus stops aren't in the dataset, so this layer undercounts bus access. Read it as "rail or bus station", not "any bus".
      • Reach isn't capacity. A clinic 5 minutes away might be full, expensive or closed at night.

      Data and how it's built

      Places: Overture Maps (release 2026-10-21.0, read from S3). Population: Meta & CIESIN High Resolution Settlement Layer (CC BY 4.0, read from S3). Map, search, walking areas and routes: Amazon Location Service. Hosted on AWS Amplify.
